BHDisplay is installed 🎉

BHDisplay is now open — look for the monitor icon in the menu bar. Click it to switch inputs, or open the window for every setting. Next time, find it in your Applications folder.

First launch: macOS may say it can't verify the developer (the app isn't notarized — that needs a paid Apple account). Go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Monitor setup: turn on Setup Menu ▸ DDC/CI on the monitor, and connect the Mac with its HDMI port or a USB-C to DisplayPort cable — many USB-C-to-HDMI adapters block monitor control.

Free & open-source: github.com/wpexpertinbd/BHDisplay — thank you for using BHDisplay!
