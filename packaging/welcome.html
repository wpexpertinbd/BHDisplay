BHDisplay __VERSION__

Switch your ViewSonic monitor between your Mac and another computer — and control it — right from the macOS menu bar.

This installer places BHDisplay in your Applications folder. It gives you:

  • One-click input switching — DisplayPort, HDMI 1, HDMI 2
  • Finds which port your Mac is on by itself; ⌃⌥⌘S flips Mac ⇄ the other computer
  • Brightness, contrast, sharpness, blue light filter and volume sliders
  • View Mode, colour temperature and the monitor's Auto Detect switch
  • Keyboard & mouse sharing with BHDisplay for Windows — either keyboard works on both computers
  • Lives in the menu bar and can start at login — no Dock icon in your way
100% free & open-source — built with ❤️ by BiswasHost (Benjamin Biswas).
