BHDisplay — by BiswasHost
=========================

Switch your ViewSonic monitor between your Mac and another computer (Windows
PC, console…) from the macOS menu bar, and control brightness, contrast,
sharpness, blue light filter, volume, View Mode and colour temperature —
without touching the monitor's buttons. 100% free & open-source.


INSTALL
-------

1) Drag the BHDisplay icon onto the Applications folder (shown here).

2) FIRST LAUNCH — allowing the app:
   BHDisplay is signed but not notarized by Apple (notarization needs a paid
   Apple account), so macOS will say it "could not verify BHDisplay is free
   of malware." This is normal. Approve it once:

     a. Open BHDisplay from your Applications folder. macOS blocks it — click
        "Done".
     b. Open  System Settings  ›  Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down to  "BHDisplay was blocked…"  and click  "Open Anyway".
     d. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.

   BHDisplay opens and will not ask again.

3) MONITOR SETUP:
   - On the monitor: Setup Menu › DDC/CI › On.
   - Connect the Mac with its HDMI port, or a USB-C to DisplayPort cable.
     Many USB-C to HDMI adapters pass the picture but block monitor control.
   - Apple Silicon Macs only (M1 or newer).
